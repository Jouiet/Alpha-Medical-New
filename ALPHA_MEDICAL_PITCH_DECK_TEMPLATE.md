   - "Great question" (even if hostile)

Our advantage is curation and verification. Every supplier is ISO 13485 certified. We're the anti-Amazon - quality over quantity. Plus, Amazon takes 15% commission from sellers. We buy direct from manufacturers at 40% below retail. Even if Amazon matched our selection, they can't match our margins or trust level."

**GOOD ANSWER:**
> "Two ways: First, our suppliers are B2B manufacturers - they don't have the infrastructure for DTC sales (customer service, marketing, branding). They want to focus on manufacturing, not ecommerce. **GOOD ANSWER:**
