# COMPARISON TEMPLATE
**Type:** Product Comparison Video (Us vs Them)
**Duration:** 25-30 seconds optimal
**Format:** 1:1 square (1080x1080) OR 9:16 vertical (1080x1920)
**Best For:** Competitive categories, premium positioning, skeptical audiences
**UGC Score:** Medium (educational + persuasive, less emotional than UGC)
**Platform Priority:** Facebook Feed > Instagram Reels > TikTok

---

## 🎬 COMPLETE SCRIPT STRUCTURE

### [0-4 sec] HOOK - Problem with Alternatives

**Opening Hook Patterns:**

**Pattern 1: "Stop buying..." (Negative command + problem)**
Examples:
- "Stop buying cheap knee braces that fall apart in a month..."
- "Stop wasting money on posture correctors that dig into your skin..."
- "Stop settling for basic back supports with no real reinforcement..."

**Pattern 2: "Most [products]..." (Category criticism)**
Examples:
- "Most knee braces are either too bulky or provide zero support..."
- "Most posture correctors are uncomfortable after 10 minutes..."
- "Most back supports use cheap elastic that loses compression fast..."

**Pattern 3: "Why choose..." (Direct comparison setup)**
Examples:
- "Why choose our magnetic knee pads over standard braces?"
- "Why pay more for our posture corrector? Let me show you..."
- "Why is our back brace worth $20 more than drugstore options?"

**Pattern 4: "There's a reason..." (Authority positioning)**
Examples:
- "There's a reason doctors recommend ours over cheaper alternatives..."
- "There's a reason we have 4.8 stars and competitors have 3.2..."
- "There's a reason 80% of customers switch to us and never go back..."

**Hook Selection by Competitive Landscape:**
- **Crowded market (many competitors):** Pattern 1 or 2 (differentiate aggressively)
- **Price-sensitive audience:** Pattern 3 (justify premium)
- **Quality-focused audience:** Pattern 4 (authority positioning)

**Visual Direction for Creatify:**
- Avatar: Slightly frustrated OR knowing/confident expression
- Setting: Clean, professional (credibility)
- Product: OUR product visible early (brand visibility)
- Text overlay: Optional "⚠️ Before you buy..." or "Watch this first"

---

### [5-12 sec] COMPARISON POINTS - Feature-by-Feature

**Comparison Structure: 3-Point Format**

**POINT 1: Material/Quality (Most Important)**

**Script Pattern:**
```
"Standard braces: {INFERIOR_MATERIAL} that {NEGATIVE_OUTCOME}.
Our {PRODUCT}: {SUPERIOR_MATERIAL} that {POSITIVE_OUTCOME}."
```

**Examples:**
- "Drugstore knee braces: thin neoprene that loses elasticity in weeks. Ours: medical-grade compression fabric with reinforced stitching that lasts years."
- "Cheap posture correctors: scratchy elastic that digs into skin. Ours: breathable mesh with padded straps you can wear all day."
- "Basic back supports: single-layer elastic with no structure. Ours: dual-layer compression with 6 steel stays for real lumbar support."

**Visual Direction:**
- Split screen OR side-by-side comparison
- Left: Generic/competitor product (no brand visible)
- Right: OUR product (clear branding)
- Text overlay: "❌ Standard" (left) vs "✅ Ours" (right)

---

**POINT 2: Features/Technology**

**Script Pattern:**
```
"Typical [products]: {MISSING_FEATURE}.
Our {PRODUCT}: {UNIQUE_FEATURE} that {SPECIFIC_BENEFIT}."
```

**Examples:**
- "Regular knee braces: no heating, no therapy. Ours: self-heating tourmaline nodes + 12 magnetic therapy points for active pain relief."
- "Other posture correctors: just pull your shoulders back. Ours: 8 magnetic nodes along spine to retrain muscle memory naturally."
- "Standard supports: one-size-fits-all that fits no one. Ours: 4-point adjustable compression for custom fit every time."

**Visual Direction:**
- Feature close-up: Show OUR unique feature (nodes, stays, adjustability)
- Comparison graphic: Simple visual chart (Them vs Us)
- Text overlay: Feature highlight (e.g., "Self-Heating Technology ❄️→🔥")

---

**POINT 3: Results/Longevity**

**Script Pattern:**
```
"With cheap [products]: {SHORT-TERM_PROBLEM}.
With ours: {LONG-TERM_BENEFIT}. {PROOF_ELEMENT}."
```

**Examples:**
- "With cheap braces: pain relief for a week, then back to square one. With ours: lasting support that works for years. 8,000+ customers, 4.8 stars."
- "With flimsy correctors: you stop wearing them after a few days. With ours: comfortable enough to wear 8+ hours daily. 85% still use after 6 months."
- "With basic supports: you'll buy 3-4 replacements per year. With ours: one purchase, lifetime durability. 30-day money-back guarantee."

**Visual Direction:**
- Timeline graphic: Short-term vs Long-term
- Customer testimonial quote overlay (if available)
- Star rating visual: Ours 4.8⭐ vs Competitors 3.2⭐
- Text overlay: Social proof number (e.g., "8,000+ Happy Customers")

---

### [13-20 sec] VALUE PROPOSITION - Why Worth It

**Value Justification:**

**Pattern 1: "Yes, it's $X more, but..." (Address price objection)**
Examples:
- "Yes, it's $20 more than drugstore options, but you'll never need to replace it."
- "Yes, it costs more upfront, but compare: their $30 brace lasts 2 months. Ours lasts 2+ years."
- "Higher price, yes. But you're paying for medical-grade quality, not disposable junk."

**Pattern 2: "Think about it..." (ROI calculation)**
Examples:
- "Think about it: $60 once vs. $30 every few months. Which is actually cheaper?"
- "Think about it: would you rather pay $40 for real relief or $15 for something that doesn't work?"
- "Do the math: their $25 brace × 4 replacements per year = $100. Ours: $60 once. You save $40."

**Pattern 3: "Invest in..." (Reframe as investment)**
Examples:
- "Invest in your health with a product that actually works."
- "Invest once in quality instead of repeatedly buying cheap failures."
- "Invest in something that lasts instead of temporary fixes."

**Visual Direction:**
- Avatar: Confident, holding OUR product
- Price comparison graphic: Show cost-per-use or annual cost
- Text overlay: Value equation (e.g., "$60 once vs $120/year")
- Product: Final hero shot of OUR product

---

### [21-25 sec] SOCIAL PROOF & GUARANTEE

**Proof Stacking:**

**Script Pattern:**
```
"{CERTIFICATION/AUTHORITY}. {SOCIAL_PROOF_NUMBER}. {RISK_REVERSAL}."
```

**Examples:**
- "FDA-registered. Over 10,000 verified customers. 30-day money-back guarantee if you're not thrilled."
- "Doctor-recommended. 4.8 stars from 3,000+ reviews. Try it risk-free for 30 days."
- "Medical-grade certified. Trusted by physiotherapists nationwide. Love it or return it, no questions asked."

**Visual Direction:**
- Certification badges: FDA, CE, medical-grade logos
- Star rating: Large, prominent ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.8/5
- Guarantee seal: "30-Day Money-Back Guarantee" badge
- Text overlay: Stack all proof elements together

---

### [26-30 sec] CALL TO ACTION

**CTA Patterns:**

**Pattern 1: "Make the switch..." (Competitive action)**
- "Make the switch to quality. Get 20% off today with code UPGRADE20."
- "Stop settling. Upgrade to the best. Link in bio."
- "Make the smart choice. Order now at alphamedical.shop."

**Pattern 2: "Limited offer..." (Urgency)**
- "Limited stock available. Don't miss out. Code COMPARE20."
- "Sale ends Sunday. Grab yours before it's too late."
- "Only 47 left in stock. Order now."

**Pattern 3: "Join thousands..." (Social proof CTA)**
- "Join 10,000+ customers who made the switch. Link below."
- "See why thousands choose us over cheap alternatives."
- "Join the 4.8-star community. Order today."

**Text Overlay (Last 4-5 seconds - MANDATORY):**
```
alphamedical.shop
-20% CODE: {COMPARISON_CODE}
```

**Overlay Styling:**

## 🎭 CREATIFY CONFIGURATION GUIDE

### Avatar Selection (Credible Authority Figure)

**Key Principle:** Trustworthy expert, not aggressive salesperson

**For Premium Positioning (Justify Higher Price):**
- Age: 40-55 (mature, experienced)
- Appearance: Business casual or smart casual (button-down, blazer optional)
- Setting: Professional home office or clean background
- Demeanor: Confident, knowledgeable, slightly assertive (not pushy)
- Expression: Knowing smile (you know the secret competitors won't tell you)

**For Quality-Focused Messaging:**
- Age: 45-60 (seasoned, seen it all)
- Appearance: Professional casual (polo, cardigan, glasses = authority)
- Setting: Well-lit home or office (credibility)
- Demeanor: Helpful educator, insider sharing wisdom
- Expression: Sincere, trustworthy, slight concern for viewer's wellbeing

**For Value/ROI Positioning:**
- Age: 35-50 (savvy shopper, practical)
- Appearance: Casual professional (relatable, not corporate)
- Setting: Home or realistic everyday setting
- Demeanor: Friendly advisor, honest comparison
- Expression: "I'm telling you this as a friend" vibe

**Search Terms Creatify:**
- "confident professional woman business casual indoor"
- "trustworthy presenter mature office setting"
- "knowledgeable man explaining product serious helpful"
- "expert advisor friendly professional background"

**AVOID:**
- Overly formal (feels like corporate ad)
- Too casual (reduces credibility for premium positioning)
- Aggressive expression (comparison should educate, not attack)

---

### Voice Selection

**Tone Characteristics:**
- Gender: Neutral or match target demographic
- Age: Mature (40-60) - experienced authority
- Emotion: Confident + helpful (not condescending)
- Pace: Medium (clear articulation of comparisons)

**Voice Style:**
- **Opening (hook):** Slightly critical but constructive (frustrated with inferior options)
- **Comparison:** Clear, factual, matter-of-fact (state facts, not opinions)
- **Value prop:** Confident, reassuring (this is the smart choice)
- **CTA:** Warm invitation, no pressure (offer is there when ready)

**Creatify Voice Filters:**
- Language: English (US) - neutral, educated accent
- Tone: Professional conversational
- Energy: Medium (authoritative but approachable)
- Clarity: Very high (comparisons must be crystal clear)

**Search Terms Creatify:**
- "authoritative voice professional comparison"
- "confident presenter voice educational"
- "mature voice trustworthy product review"

**AVOID:**
- Overly enthusiastic (reduces credibility)
- Monotone (boring for 30 sec)
- Condescending tone (alienates competitor users)

---

### Captions Configuration

**MANDATORY for Comparisons:**
✅ Captions ENABLED (complex information, fact retention critical)

**Settings:**
- Style: White text + black background (high contrast essential)
- Font: Bold sans-serif (Montserrat Bold, Roboto Bold)
- Position: Bottom third (standard) OR top for comparison labels
- Timing: Perfect sync <0.2 sec (precision = credibility)
- Line breaks: Max 2 lines, 4-6 words per line

**Caption Strategy for Comparisons:**
- **Competitor mentions:** Regular weight or gray text (de-emphasize)
- **Our features:** BOLD or color highlight (emphasize)
- **Numbers:** Always highlight (e.g., "4.8 stars" vs "3.2 stars")

**Platform-Specific:**
- **Facebook:** LARGER captions (older demographic, comparison facts must be readable)
- **Instagram:** Standard size, professional style
- **TikTok:** Auto-highlight if available (word-by-word emphasis)

## 🔄 A/B TESTING VARIATIONS

### Version A: Feature Comparison (Rational Buyers)
- Emphasize: Specifications, materials, technology
- Hook: "Why choose ours over cheaper options? Three reasons..."
- Best for: Educated audience, research-oriented buyers

### Version B: Price/Value Comparison (Budget-Conscious)
- Emphasize: Cost-per-use, ROI, savings calculation
- Hook: "Think ours is expensive? Do the math..."
- Best for: Price-sensitive audience, repeat buyers of cheap products

### Version C: Results Comparison (Outcome-Focused)
- Emphasize: What you GET, not what it HAS
- Hook: "Cheap braces gave me 2 weeks of relief. This gave me 2 years..."
- Best for: Frustrated buyers, previous product failures

### Version D: Social Proof Comparison (Skeptical Buyers)
- Emphasize: Reviews, ratings, testimonials
- Hook: "4.8 stars vs 3.2 stars. There's a reason..."
- Best for: Risk-averse buyers, first-time purchasers

**Test different angles with same product.**

## 📊 PERFORMANCE OPTIMIZATION

### Hook Testing (First 4 Seconds Critical)

**Test 1:** "Stop buying..." (Negative command)
- Expected 3-sec view rate: 70-75%
- Best for: Frustrated repeat buyers

**Test 2:** "Most [products]..." (Category criticism)
- Expected 3-sec view rate: 65-70%
- Best for: Educated, skeptical audience

**Test 3:** "There's a reason..." (Authority)
- Expected 3-sec view rate: 60-65%
- Best for: Risk-averse, research-oriented

### Comparison Style Optimization

**Side-by-side visual:**
- Higher engagement, clearer contrast
- Better for: Visual differences (size, design)

**Sequential comparison:**
- Better for: Feature/benefit differences
- More conversational, less aggressive

**Optimal:** Combine both - visual for design, sequential for features

- Format: 9:16 vertical

## 💡 ADVANCED PSYCHOLOGY TIPS

1. **Avoid Direct Brand Attacks:**
   - Say "cheap alternatives" NOT "Brand X sucks"
   - Say "standard braces" NOT "competitor products are garbage"
   - Constructive comparison > aggressive attack

2. **Use "You" Language:**
   - "You'll save $125" > "Customers save money"
   - "You won't need to replace it" > "It lasts longer"
   - Personal relevance = higher conversion

3. **Specificity Builds Trust:**
   - "4.8 stars from 3,247 reviews" > "highly rated"
   - "$55 vs $180 per year" > "more affordable long-term"
   - Exact numbers = credible comparison

4. **Risk Reversal Closes Sales:**
   - "30-day money-back guarantee" = removes purchase fear
   - "Try it risk-free" = lowers barrier
   - Always include guarantee in comparison ads

5. **Social Proof Validates Claims:**
   - "12,000 customers switched" = bandwagon effect
   - "85% still using after 6 months" = retention proof
   - Numbers validate comparison claims
