# PRODUCT DEMO TEMPLATE
**Type:** Product Demonstration Video
**Duration:** 18-25 seconds optimal
**Format:** 9:16 vertical (1080x1920) OR 1:1 square (1080x1080)
**Best For:** Products with unique features, technology, or visible mechanisms
**UGC Score:** Medium-High (educational + authentic presentation)
**Platform Priority:** TikTok > Instagram Reels > Facebook Reels

---

## 🎬 COMPLETE SCRIPT STRUCTURE

### [0-3 sec] HOOK - Feature Revelation

**Opening Hook Patterns:**

**Pattern 1: "Wait, this..." (Curiosity interruption)**
Examples:
- "Wait, this knee brace heats itself without batteries..."
- "Wait, this back support has 6 steel stays..."
- "Wait, this posture corrector is invisible under clothes..."

**Pattern 2: "You need to see..." (Direct invitation)**
Examples:
- "You need to see how this self-heating technology works..."
- "You need to see what makes this different from every other brace..."
- "You need to see the magnetic therapy nodes up close..."

**Pattern 3: "I can't believe..." (Amazement)**
Examples:
- "I can't believe how lightweight this knee brace is..."
- "I can't believe this actually heats up on its own..."
- "I can't believe I can wear this under my shirt all day..."

**Pattern 4: "This is not..." (Contrast positioning)**
Examples:
- "This is not your typical knee brace. Watch this..."
- "This is not like other posture correctors. Here's why..."
- "This is not just another back support. Let me show you..."

**Hook Selection by Product Feature:**
- **Technology-Based (self-heating, magnetic):** Pattern 1 or 2
- **Design Innovation (invisible, lightweight):** Pattern 3 or 4
- **Medical-Grade Features (steel stays, hinged):** Pattern 2 or 4

**Visual Direction for Creatify:**
- Avatar: Holding product OR product prominently displayed
- Expression: Genuine interest/excitement (not salesy)
- Setting: Clean background (product is focus)
- Lighting: Bright, clear (showcase product details)

---

### [4-8 sec] FEATURE 1 - Primary USP Demonstration

**Feature Introduction:**

**Pattern 1: "See this..." (Visual demonstration)**
- "See these tourmaline nodes? They generate natural heat using your body's warmth."
- "See these 6 steel stays? Medical-grade support that adapts to your spine."
- "See how thin this is? Completely invisible under any clothing."

**Pattern 2: "What makes this special is..." (Educational)**
- "What makes this special is the self-heating technology. No batteries, no charging."
- "What makes this different is the adjustable compression. Custom fit every time."
- "What makes this work is the magnetic therapy nodes. 12 strategically placed magnets."

**Pattern 3: "Unlike other [products]..." (Comparison positioning)**
- "Unlike other knee braces, this heats itself naturally."
- "Unlike typical back supports, this has medical-grade steel reinforcement."
- "Unlike bulky correctors, this is invisible and comfortable all day."

**Visual Direction:**
- Product close-up: Show ACTUAL feature (nodes, stays, material)
- Avatar interaction: Touching/pointing to specific feature
- Text overlay: Feature name (e.g., "Self-Heating Technology ❄️→🔥")
- Duration: 4-5 seconds (enough to comprehend feature)

**Demonstration Angles:**
- Technology features: Macro close-up (show detail)
- Material features: Fabric feel, flexibility demonstration
- Structural features: Side view, cross-section explanation

---

### [9-14 sec] FEATURE 2 - Secondary USP or Usage

**Usage Demonstration:**

**Pattern 1: "And here's the best part..." (Benefit reveal)**
- "And here's the best part: it stays in place during any activity."
- "And the best part: you can adjust it in seconds for perfect fit."
- "And here's what I love: I forget I'm even wearing it."

**Pattern 2: "You can..." (Application showcase)**
- "You can wear this while walking, sitting, or sleeping."
- "You can adjust the compression with one hand."
- "You can wear this under work clothes, workout gear, anything."

**Pattern 3: "It works by..." (Mechanism explanation)**
- "It works by using your body heat to activate tourmaline minerals."
- "It works by providing targeted compression exactly where you need it."
- "It works by gently pulling your shoulders back into natural alignment."

**Visual Direction:**
- Lifestyle context: Show product in use (avatar wearing it)
- Movement demonstration: Walking, bending, sitting
- Versatility showcase: Different settings (office, gym, outdoor)
- Text overlay: Secondary benefit (e.g., "Adjustable Fit 🎯")

---

### [15-19 sec] FEATURE 3 - Proof/Credibility Element

**Credibility Builders:**

**Pattern 1: "Certified..." (Authority)**
- "FDA-registered. Doctor-recommended. Trusted by thousands."
- "Medical-grade materials. CE certified. Safe for daily use."
- "Tested by physiotherapists. Approved for rehabilitation."

**Pattern 2: "[X] customers..." (Social proof)**
- "Over 10,000 customers see results in 2 weeks."
- "4.8 stars from 3,000+ verified reviews."
- "Recommended by orthopedic specialists nationwide."

**Pattern 3: "Works for..." (Versatility proof)**
- "Works for arthritis, sports injuries, and chronic pain."
- "Perfect for seniors, athletes, and office workers."
- "Effective for prevention and recovery both."

**Visual Direction:**
- Certification badges: Quick flash of FDA, CE logos
- Star ratings: Visual representation of reviews
- Diverse users: Show different demographics (if applicable)
- Text overlay: Proof statement (e.g., "10K+ Happy Customers ⭐")

---

### [20-23 sec] CALL TO ACTION

**CTA Patterns:**

**Pattern 1: "Get yours..." (Direct invitation)**
- "Get yours today. Link in bio. Limited stock."
- "Get 20% off right now. Code DEMO20. Link below."
- "Get yours at alphamedical.shop. Free shipping over $50."

**Pattern 2: "Try it risk-free..." (Low-barrier offer)**
- "Try it risk-free for 30 days. You'll love it."
- "30-day money-back guarantee. Zero risk."
- "Free returns. If it doesn't work, send it back."

**Pattern 3: "Don't wait..." (Urgency)**
- "Don't wait. Winter sale ends soon. Grab yours now."
- "Limited stock available. Order today."
- "Sale ends in 48 hours. Link in bio."

**Text Overlay (Last 3-4 seconds):**
```
alphamedical.shop
-20% CODE: {PRODUCT_CODE}
FREE SHIPPING $50+
```

**Overlay Styling:**
- Font: Bold sans-serif (Arial Black, Montserrat Bold)
- Color: White text + semi-transparent black background
- Position: Bottom third (mobile-safe zone)
- Animation: Gentle fade-in (not distracting)

## 🎭 CREATIFY CONFIGURATION GUIDE

### Avatar Selection (Product Demo Specialists)

**Key Principle:** Credible presenter, not salesperson

**For Technology Products (Self-Heating, Magnetic):**
- Age: 35-50 (mature, knowledgeable)
- Appearance: Smart casual (button-down, glasses optional = authority)
- Setting: Clean home or office (minimal distraction)
- Demeanor: Educational, confident, genuine interest

**For Medical-Grade Products (Braces, Supports):**
- Age: 40-60 (experienced, trustworthy)
- Appearance: Professional casual (polo, sweater)
- Setting: Well-lit indoor (home office, living room)
- Demeanor: Helpful, empathetic, authoritative

**For Lifestyle Products (Everyday comfort):**
- Age: 30-45 (relatable, active)
- Appearance: Athletic casual (hoodie, workout top)
- Setting: Home or gym (realistic usage context)
- Demeanor: Enthusiastic but authentic, personal experience

**Search Terms Creatify:**
- "mature professional demonstrating product indoor"
- "confident presenter home office clean background"
- "trustworthy person explaining product casual"
- "knowledgeable woman holding item clear lighting"

**Avatar Body Language:**
- Hands: Interacting with product (holding, pointing, adjusting)
- Eyes: Looking at product then camera (engaging viewer)
- Expression: Interested, impressed (genuine, not fake excitement)
- Posture: Upright, professional, approachable

---

### Voice Selection

**Tone Characteristics:**
- Gender: Neutral or match target demographic
- Age: Mature (35-55) - credible educator
- Emotion: Informative + enthusiastic (not salesy)
- Pace: Medium to slightly faster (energetic but clear)

**Voice Style by Product:**
- **Technology products:** Confident, clear, slightly faster (modern feel)
- **Medical products:** Warm, reassuring, medium pace (trustworthy)
- **Lifestyle products:** Friendly, conversational, natural pace (relatable)

**Creatify Voice Filters:**
- Language: English (US) - clear neutral accent
- Tone: Educational conversational
- Energy: Medium-High (engaged, not hyper)
- Clarity: High (enunciation important for features)

**Search Terms Creatify:**
- "educational presenter voice clear confident"
- "product demonstration voice professional friendly"
- "mature explainer voice warm authoritative"

**Voice Variation by Section:**
- HOOK (0-3 sec): Slightly higher energy (grab attention)
- FEATURE 1 (4-8 sec): Clear, informative (explain)
- FEATURE 2 (9-14 sec): Conversational, relatable (application)
- PROOF (15-19 sec): Authoritative, confident (credibility)
- CTA (20-23 sec): Warm invitation, friendly (not pushy)

---

### Captions Configuration

**MANDATORY for Product Demos:**
✅ Captions ENABLED (complex info needs visual reinforcement)

**Settings:**
- Style: White text + black background OR auto-highlight style
- Font: Bold sans-serif (Montserrat, Roboto Bold)
- Position: Bottom third (standard) OR middle (for emphasis)
- Timing: Perfect sync <0.2 seconds (technical info must be precise)
- Line breaks: Max 2 lines, 3-5 words per line

**Caption Emphasis Strategy:**
- FEATURE NAMES: All caps or bold (e.g., "SELF-HEATING TECHNOLOGY")
- Numbers: Highlight distinctly (e.g., "6 STEEL STAYS", "12 MAGNETS")
- Benefits: Color emphasis if platform allows (red for pain, green for relief)

**Platform-Specific Caption Styles:**
- **TikTok:** Auto-highlight captions (word-by-word yellow highlight)
- **Instagram:** Classic white/black box (professional, clean)
- **Facebook:** Larger font (older demographic, readability priority)

## 🔄 A/B TESTING VARIATIONS

### Version A: Feature-Heavy (Tech-Savvy Audience)
- Emphasize: Mechanisms, materials, specifications
- Hook: "You need to see how this works..."
- Best for: Younger demographics (25-45), high education

### Version B: Benefit-Focused (Results-Oriented Audience)
- Emphasize: What it does for YOU, outcomes
- Hook: "This changed how I [activity]..."
- Best for: Older demographics (55+), practical users

### Version C: Comparison-Based (Skeptical Audience)
- Emphasize: "Unlike other products..."
- Hook: "This is not your typical [product]..."
- Best for: Experienced buyers, previous product failures

**Test with same product, different emphasis angles.**

## 📊 PERFORMANCE OPTIMIZATION

### Hook Testing (First 3 Seconds Critical)

**Test 1:** "Wait, this..." (Pattern interrupt)
- Expected 3-sec view rate: 75-80%
- Best for: Unique technology features

**Test 2:** "You need to see..." (Direct invitation)
- Expected 3-sec view rate: 70-75%
- Best for: Visual demonstrations

**Test 3:** "This is not..." (Contrast)
- Expected 3-sec view rate: 65-70%
- Best for: Crowded product categories

### Feature Presentation Optimization

**Quick cuts (2-3 sec per feature):**
- Better for: Short attention spans, TikTok
- Higher completion rate, less detail retention

**Slower demonstration (5-6 sec per feature):**
- Better for: Educational content, older demographics
- Lower completion but higher understanding

**Optimal balance:** 4-5 seconds per key feature

## 💡 ADVANCED PSYCHOLOGY TIPS

1. **Show, Don't Just Tell:**
   - Physical demonstration > verbal description
   - Close-ups of features build credibility
   - Hand interaction (touching product) = tangible reality

2. **Repetition for Retention:**
   - Mention key feature in voice + caption + text overlay
   - Triple reinforcement = 3x retention rate

3. **Number Specificity = Trust:**
   - "12 magnetic nodes" > "multiple magnets"
   - "6 steel stays" > "reinforced support"
   - "8,000 customers" > "thousands of users"

4. **Contrast Creates Value:**
   - "Unlike other braces..." positions as premium
   - "This is not..." creates curiosity
   - "Instead of batteries..." highlights innovation

5. **Proof Stacking:**
   - Certification + Reviews + Usage stats
   - Triple proof = credibility spike

## 🛠️ PRODUCTION TIPS

### Asset Preparation Priority

**MUST HAVE:**
1. Hero image (white background, high res)
