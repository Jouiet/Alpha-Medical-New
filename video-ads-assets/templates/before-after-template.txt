# BEFORE/AFTER TRANSFORMATION TEMPLATE
**Type:** Before/After Comparison Video
**Duration:** 12-15 seconds optimal
**Format:** 1:1 square (1080x1080) OR 9:16 vertical (1080x1920)
**Best For:** Products with VISIBLE transformation (posture, pain relief, mobility)
**UGC Score:** High (shows real results, builds trust)
**Platform Priority:** Instagram Reels > Facebook Feed > TikTok

---

## 🎬 COMPLETE SCRIPT STRUCTURE

### [0-2 sec] BEFORE STATE - Show the Problem

**Opening Hook Patterns:**

**Pattern 1: "Before..." (Simple past tense)**
Examples:
- "Before this brace, I couldn't even stand up straight..."
- "Before these knee pads, stairs were my nightmare..."
- "Before this posture corrector, I had constant headaches..."

**Pattern 2: "Look at this..." (Direct visual)**
Examples:
- "Look at my posture 3 weeks ago... hunched, painful..."
- "Look at how I walked before... limping, slow..."
- "Look at my mobility before... stiff, limited..."

**Pattern 3: "[Time period] ago..." (Timeline emphasis)**
Examples:
- "30 days ago, I couldn't lift my arms above my head..."
- "2 months ago, my back looked like this..."
- "3 weeks ago, I couldn't sit without pain..."

**Hook Selection by Product Type:**
- **Posture Products:** Pattern 2 or 3 (visual is KEY)
- **Pain Relief:** Pattern 1 or 3 (emphasize duration)
- **Mobility:** Pattern 1 or 2 (show limitation)

**Visual Direction for Creatify:**
- Avatar: Neutral to slightly concerned expression
- Setting: Same setting for BEFORE and AFTER (consistency = credibility)
- Body language: Slouched, restricted, uncomfortable (subtle, not dramatic)
- Clothing: Same outfit BEFORE and AFTER (reinforces authenticity)

---

### [3-6 sec] THE TRANSITION - Introduce Product

**Transition Phrases:**

**Pattern 1: "Then I tried..." (Discovery moment)**
- "Then I tried {PRODUCT_NAME}..."
- "Then I discovered this {PRODUCT_CATEGORY} from {BRAND}..."
- "Then I started wearing {PRODUCT_NAME} daily..."

**Pattern 2: "After using..." (Result-focused)**
- "After using {PRODUCT_NAME} for {TIME_PERIOD}..."
- "After wearing this for just {X} weeks..."
- "After {X} days with {PRODUCT_NAME}..."

**Time Period Guidelines:**
- Posture correction: "3 weeks" or "30 days" (realistic timeframe)
- Pain relief: "2 weeks" or "14 days" (credible, not too fast)
- Mobility improvement: "1 month" or "4 weeks" (substantial change)

**Visual Direction:**
- Product image: Show ACTUAL product (hero image)
- Avatar: Slight smile, hopeful expression
- Text overlay: Product name + time period (e.g., "After 30 days with Magnetic Posture Corrector")

---

### [7-10 sec] AFTER STATE - Show Transformation

**Result Statements:**

**Pattern 1: "Now..." (Present transformation)**
- "Now I stand tall without even thinking about it."
- "Now I can walk 3 miles without pain."
- "Now my shoulders are aligned naturally."

**Pattern 2: "Look at the difference..." (Visual proof)**
- "Look at the difference. My posture completely changed."
- "Look at this. No more slouching, no more pain."
- "Look at my alignment now. Night and day."

**Pattern 3: "{SPECIFIC_BENEFIT}" (Measurable result)**
- "2 inches taller. No back pain. Confident posture."
- "Pain-free for 30 days straight. Life changing."
- "Full range of motion. I can finally exercise again."

**Visual Direction for Creatify:**
- Avatar: Confident smile, upright posture
- Setting: SAME as BEFORE (critical for credibility)
- Body language: Open, relaxed, natural alignment
- Lighting: Slightly brighter (subtle psychological cue)

---

### [11-12 sec] CALL TO ACTION

**CTA Patterns:**

**Pattern 1: "If you struggle with..." (Relate to viewer)**
- "If you struggle with back pain, try this. Link in bio."
- "If you have posture issues, this changed my life. Link below."

**Pattern 2: "Best investment..." (Value emphasis)**
- "Best $60 I ever spent. Link in bio."
- "Totally worth it. Get yours at {WEBSITE}."

**Pattern 3: "You won't regret it" (Confidence)**
- "You won't regret this. Link in bio."
- "Changed my life. It can change yours too."

**Text Overlay (Last 2-3 seconds):**
```
alphamedical.shop
-{DISCOUNT}% CODE: {DISCOUNT_CODE}
```

**Overlay Styling:**
- Font: Bold sans-serif (Arial Black, Helvetica Bold)
- Color: White text + black background rectangle
- Position: Bottom third (safe zone for all platforms)
- Size: Large enough to read on mobile (min 48pt)

## 🎭 CREATIFY CONFIGURATION GUIDE

### Avatar Selection (CRITICAL for Before/After Credibility)

**Key Principle:** SAME avatar, DIFFERENT posture/expression

**For Posture Corrector Products:**
- Age: 30-45 (office worker demographic)
- Appearance: Professional casual (button-down, sweater)
- Setting: Office desk or home workspace
- BEFORE pose: Slouched, rounded shoulders
- AFTER pose: Upright, shoulders back

**For Pain Relief Products (Back/Knee):**
- Age: 55-70 (seniors with chronic pain)
- Appearance: Casual comfortable (polo, cardigan)
- Setting: Living room or outdoor park
- BEFORE pose: Restricted movement, cautious
- AFTER pose: Relaxed movement, confident

**For Mobility Products:**
- Age: 40-60 (active adults)
- Appearance: Athletic casual (performance wear)
- Setting: Home gym or outdoor
- BEFORE pose: Limited range of motion
- AFTER pose: Full extension, fluid movement

**Search Terms Creatify BEFORE Avatar:**
- "middle-aged person slouching desk"
- "senior cautious movement indoor"
- "adult restricted posture office"

**Search Terms Creatify AFTER Avatar:**
- "confident upright posture professional"
- "senior active outdoor walking"
- "adult natural posture relaxed"

**PRO TIP:** If Creatify doesn't allow posture change for same avatar:
- Use split-screen effect in editing
- OR use VERY similar-looking avatars (same age, ethnicity, setting)
- Consistency in background/clothing is MORE important than exact same face

---

### Voice Selection

**Tone Characteristics:**
- Gender: Match target demographic (female for seniors/wellness, male for athletes)
- Age: Mature (35-55 range) - credible, experienced
- Emotion: START neutral/concerned → END confident/happy
- Pace: Medium (not rushed, sincere)

**Creatify Voice Filters:**
- Language: English (US) - neutral accent
- Tone: Conversational, sincere
- Energy: Medium (not overly enthusiastic)
- Warmth: High (trustworthy)

**Search Terms Creatify:**
- "mature female sincere transformation"
- "mature male confident testimonial"
- "authentic voice before-after story"

**Voice Variation by Section:**
- BEFORE (0-2 sec): Lower energy, slightly slower
- TRANSITION (3-6 sec): Rising energy, hopeful
- AFTER (7-10 sec): Higher energy, enthusiastic (but not fake)
- CTA (11-12 sec): Warm invitation, friendly

---

### Captions Configuration

**MANDATORY for Before/After:**
✅ Captions ENABLED (85% watch without sound)

**Settings:**
- Style: White text + black background box (high contrast)
- Font: Bold sans-serif (maximum readability)
- Position: Bottom third (allows product image visibility)
- Timing: Sync <0.3 seconds delay
- Line breaks: Max 2 lines, 3-4 words per line

**Caption Emphasis by Section:**
- BEFORE: Emphasize problem words ("pain", "couldn't", "struggled")
- AFTER: Emphasize transformation words ("now", "pain-free", "confident")

---

### Background Music

**Music Type:** Inspirational uplifting (transformation journey)

**Volume Settings:**
- Overall: -18dB to -20dB (voice is PRIORITY)
- BEFORE section: -20dB (subtle, somber undertone)
- AFTER section: -16dB (slightly louder, triumphant)

**Genre by Product:**
- Posture Corrector: Soft corporate motivational
- Pain Relief: Gentle acoustic hopeful
- Mobility: Upbeat soft pop

**Creatify Music Library Search:**
- "inspirational soft background"
- "transformation journey music"

**AVOID:**

- Lyrics (competes with script)
- Dramatic crescendos (feels fake)

## 💡 ADVANCED PSYCHOLOGY TIPS

1. **Timeline Credibility:**
   - Use ODD numbers (31 days vs 30 days) = feels more authentic
   - Use REALISTIC timelines (not "overnight" - less credible)

2. **Visual Consistency = Trust:**
   - SAME background BEFORE/AFTER (proves it's real)
   - SAME lighting (subtle is better than dramatic)
   - SAME clothing (reinforces authenticity)

3. **Emotion Arc:**
   - START concerned but not desperate
   - TRANSITION hopeful
   - END genuinely happy (not over-the-top)

4. **Specificity Builds Trust:**
   - "2 inches taller" > "much taller"
   - "30 days pain-free" > "no more pain"
   - "$50" > "affordable"
