## 📋 TEMPLATE OVERVIEW

**What is UGC (User-Generated Content)?**
UGC testimonial videos simulate authentic customer reviews. They feature real-looking people sharing genuine experiences with your product, not polished brand advertisements. This format dominates TikTok, Instagram Reels, and Facebook Reels because audiences trust peer recommendations over corporate messaging.

**Why This Format Works:**
- 4x higher CTR vs traditional ads (2025 data)
- 70% more engagement on Instagram vs branded content
- 57% of Gen Z prefer short authentic videos
- Stops scroll in first 3 seconds (critical for mobile)
- Works WITHOUT sound (85% mobile users)

**Performance Benchmarks (Expected):**
- CTR: 1.5-3% (good), >3% (excellent)
- CPC: $0.80-$1.50 (good), <$0.80 (excellent)
- 3-sec view rate: >60% target
- ROAS: >2.0x minimum, >3.0x scale-worthy

## 🎬 SCRIPT STRUCTURE (15 SECONDS)

### [0-3 sec] HOOK - Grab Attention IMMEDIATELY

**Purpose:** Stop the scroll within 1 second. Mobile users scroll 300+ posts/hour.

**Formula: Personal Struggle + Relatable Pain**

**{PAIN_POINT_STATEMENT}** - Replace with one of these patterns:

**Pattern 1: "I couldn't..." (Lost capability)**
Examples:
- "I couldn't run for 6 months because of knee pain..."
- "I couldn't sleep through the night without back pain..."
- "I couldn't play with my grandkids without knee stiffness..."
- "I couldn't sit at my desk for more than 2 hours..."

**Pattern 2: "My [body part] used to..." (Past suffering)**
Examples:
- "My knees used to ache every winter morning..."
- "My back used to hurt after every workday..."
- "My shoulders used to be so tense I got headaches..."
- "My feet used to swell up every evening..."

**Pattern 3: "[Condition] made..." (Condition impact)**
Examples:
- "Arthritis made simple tasks impossible..."
- "Chronic pain made me miss family events..."
- "Knee inflammation made stairs my enemy..."
- "Sciatica made driving unbearable..."

**Pattern 4: "Stop scrolling if..." (Direct interrupt)**
Examples:
- "Stop scrolling if knee pain holds you back..."
- "Stop if you wake up with back pain..."
- "Attention if arthritis limits your life..."

**Hook Selection Guide:**
- **Seniors (65+):** Pattern 2 or 3 (relate to chronic conditions)
- **Office Workers (25-55):** Pattern 1 or 2 (lost productivity)
- **Athletes (18-45):** Pattern 1 or 4 (performance impact)

**CRITICAL:** First 3 words = most important. Example:
✅ GOOD: "I couldn't run..." (clear pain point)
❌ BAD: "Have you ever experienced..." (too vague, slow)

**Visual Direction for Creatify:**
- Avatar: Slightly concerned expression (not overdramatic)
- Setting: Relatable to pain (outdoor if mobility, desk if office, home if daily)
- Body language: Subtle discomfort memory (hand on knee/back if appropriate)

---

### [4-7 sec] PROBLEM → SOLUTION - The Discovery

**Purpose:** Introduce product as the solution that changed everything.

**Formula: Transition + Product Introduction**

**Standard Script:**
"...until I discovered {PRODUCT_NAME}."

**{PRODUCT_NAME}** - Use FULL descriptive name for credibility:
✅ GOOD: "...these heated knee pads." (descriptive)
✅ GOOD: "...this magnetic posture corrector." (feature included)
❌ BAD: "...this product." (too generic)
❌ BAD: "...Alpha Medical." (brand only, not descriptive)

**Advanced Variations:**

**Variation A: Friend/Authority Recommendation**
"...until my physical therapist recommended {PRODUCT_NAME}."
"...until my friend who's a nurse told me about {PRODUCT_NAME}."
"...until I found {PRODUCT_NAME} recommended by 2,000+ seniors."

**Variation B: Research/Discovery**
"...until I discovered {PRODUCT_NAME} used in 500+ PT clinics."
"...until I found {PRODUCT_NAME} with actual medical-grade support."

**Variation C: Desperation/Last Resort**
"...until I gave {PRODUCT_NAME} one last try."
"...until I invested in professional-grade {PRODUCT_NAME}."

**Visual Direction:**
- CRITICAL: Show product here! Avatar holding product OR product image insert
- Product should be clearly visible for 2-3 seconds
- If avatar holding: Natural grip, not stiff/posed
- Lighting: Product well-lit, features visible
- Expression: Relief, discovery moment

**Creatify Product Image Options:**
1. Avatar holding product (if BYOA or appropriate stock avatar)
2. Product image overlay (upload hero image from assets)
3. Cut to product close-up (if Creatify supports B-roll)

### [8-11 sec] DEMONSTRATION - Proof of Results

**Purpose:** Prove the benefit with specific, tangible results. NOT vague claims.

**Formula: Result + USP #1 + USP #2**

**Full Script Pattern:**
"{BENEFIT_STATEMENT}. {USP_1}. {USP_2}."

**{BENEFIT_STATEMENT}** - Specific outcome achieved:

**For Pain Relief Products:**
- "Now I'm back to 5K runs. Zero pain."
- "I can finally sleep through the night."
- "Stairs are no longer my enemy."
- "I played with my grandkids pain-free all day."

**For Support/Posture Products:**
- "My posture improved in just 2 weeks."
- "I can work 8 hours without back pain now."
- "All-day comfort. I barely notice I'm wearing it."

**For Heated/Seasonal Products:**
- "Self-heating technology. Works even in -10°C."
- "Warm relief all winter long. No batteries needed."

**{USP_1}** - First unique selling point (differentiator):
- "Self-heating technology." (Tourmaline pads)
- "Six steel stays support." (Back brace)
- "Magnetic therapy nodes." (Magnetic products)
- "Medical-grade compression." (Professional products)

**{USP_2}** - Second unique selling point:
- "Works even in -10°C." (Winter products)
- "All-day comfort." (Long-wear products)
- "Professional-grade quality." (Premium positioning)
- "FDA registered device." (Credibility)

**USP Selection Guide:**

| Product Type | USP #1 Focus | USP #2 Focus |
|--------------|--------------|--------------|
| Heated/Winter | Heating technology | Temperature range |
| Support/Brace | Support structure (stays, hinges) | Comfort/wearability |
| Magnetic | Magnetic therapy | Pain relief speed |
| Compression | Compression level | Breathability |
| Posture | Correction mechanism | Discretion |

**Visual Direction:**
- Show benefit: Avatar active, smiling, moving
- Demonstrate usage: Product visible being worn
- Energy shift: From concerned (hook) to relieved/happy
- Setting change: From pain context to relief context (optional)

**Text Overlay (Creatify):**
Display at 9-10 seconds:
- Position: Top-center
- Text: "{Primary USP} [icon]"
- Examples:
  - "Self-Heating Technology ❄️→🔥"
  - "Medical-Grade Support ⭐"
  - "6 Steel Stays 🔩"
- Font: Bold, large, high contrast
- Duration: 2-3 seconds
- Animation: Fade in/out

### [12-15 sec] CTA - Call to Action + Offer

**Purpose:** Drive action with clear instructions + urgency/value.

**Formula: Action + Urgency/Value**

**Standard CTA Options:**

**Option 1: Simple Direct**
"Link in bio. You won't regret it."

**Option 2: Confidence/Guarantee**
"Try it risk-free. Link in bio."
"30-day guarantee. Link in bio."

**Option 3: Social Proof Close**
"Join 2,000+ pain-free customers. Link in bio."
"See why 500+ seniors love it. Link in bio."

**Option 4: Urgency**
"Limited stock. Get yours today."
"Sale ends soon. Link in bio."

**Option 5: Benefit Reminder**
"Your pain-free life starts here. Link in bio."
"Wake up pain-free tomorrow. Link in bio."

**CTA Selection Guide:**
- **Cold audiences:** Option 2 or 3 (reduce risk, add proof)
- **Warm audiences:** Option 1 or 4 (simpler, more direct)
- **High-priced (>$100):** Option 2 (emphasize guarantee)
- **Time-sensitive:** Option 4 (urgency)

**Visual Direction:**
- Avatar: Direct eye contact (if possible)
- Expression: Genuine recommendation, friend-to-friend
- Optional: Point to overlay text (natural gesture)
- Product: Can be visible or in background

**Text Overlay (Creatify) - MANDATORY:**
Display at 12-15 seconds:
- Position: Bottom (above captions if both present)
- Text: "alphamedical.shop | -{DISCOUNT}% CODE: {CODE}"
- Examples:
  - "alphamedical.shop | -20% CODE: WARMKNEES20"
  - "alphamedical.shop | -25% CODE: OFFICE25"
  - "alphamedical.shop | -30% CODE: WINTER30"
- Font: Bold, ultra-readable
- Colors: High contrast (white on dark background or vice versa)
- Duration: Full 3 seconds (must be readable)
- Size: Large enough for mobile

**{DISCOUNT}** - Percentage off:
- 15-20%: Standard testing
- 25%: Strong offer, office workers
- 30%: Bundle or high-ticket
- 40%+: Clearance only (devalues brand)

**{CODE}** - Discount code naming:
- Format: [CONTEXT][NUMBER]
- Examples: WARMKNEES20, OFFICE25, SENIOR20, ATHLETE25
- Max length: 15 characters (readability)
- Easy to remember + type

### Voice Selection (Tone is Everything)

**Voice Characteristics by Persona:**

**Seniors:**
- Age: Mature (45-65 voice age)
- Gender: Female often higher trust, Male for credibility (A/B test)
- Tone: Warm, reassuring, grateful
- Emotion: Relief, genuine appreciation (not salesy)
- Pace: Normal to slightly slower (0.9-1.0x)
- Accent: Neutral American or Canadian

**Creatify Voice Search:**
- Filter: Age "Mature", Tone "Warm" or "Natural"
- Test: "Conversational Female Warm" or "Mature Male Reassuring"
- Avoid: "Professional Corporate" (too formal), "Energetic Young" (mismatch)

**Office Workers:**
- Age: Mid-range (35-50 voice age)
- Tone: Professional yet friendly, relatable
- Emotion: Confident, solution-oriented
- Pace: Normal (1.0x)
- Style: Conversational, peer-to-peer

**Creatify Voice Search:**
- "Natural Conversational Woman"
- "Friendly Professional Male"

**Athletes:**
- Age: Younger (25-40 voice age)
- Tone: Energetic, dynamic, confident
- Emotion: Enthusiastic, performance-focused
- Pace: Slightly faster (1.0-1.1x)
- Style: Motivational, authentic

**Creatify Voice Search:**
- "Energetic Female"
- "Dynamic Male Confident"

**Voice Quality Check:**
- [ ] Matches avatar age/appearance
- [ ] Emotion appropriate for script
- [ ] NOT robotic or monotone
- [ ] Clear pronunciation (test medical terms)
- [ ] Pacing feels natural

**Pro Tip:** If voice sounds robotic, switch to "Natural" or "Conversational" category. Avoid "AI" or "Synthetic" labeled voices.

### Captions (MANDATORY - 85% watch without sound)

**Creatify Settings:**
```
Enable Captions: YES (non-negotiable)
Style: White text, black semi-transparent box background
Font: Sans-serif, bold weight (max readability)
Size: Medium-Large (mobile-optimized)
Position: Bottom-center (standard)
Timing: Auto-sync with voice (verify in preview)
```

**Caption Best Practices:**
- Max 2 lines on screen simultaneously
- 3-4 words per line (readability)
- Full script must be captioned (not just keywords)
- Sync timing: <0.3 second delay acceptable
- Special characters: Use emojis sparingly (❄️, 🔥, ⭐ OK)

**Quality Check:**
- [ ] All words spelled correctly (especially product name)
- [ ] Timing syncs with audio
- [ ] Readable on mobile (test 6" screen if possible)
- [ ] No truncation (all text visible)
- [ ] High contrast (white on black or inverse)

**Common Caption Errors to Fix:**
- ❌ Product name misspelled
- ❌ Discount code wrong
- ❌ Medical terms incorrect
- ❌ Too small font size
- ❌ Sync delay >1 second

## 📊 CUSTOMIZATION VARIABLES REFERENCE

### Complete Variables List

**{PAIN_POINT_STATEMENT}** - Opening hook
- Type: Question or statement
- Length: 8-12 words max
- Tone: Relatable, specific, emotional
- Example: "My knees used to ache every winter morning..."

**{PRODUCT_NAME}** - Product introduction
- Type: Descriptive product name
- Format: "these {adjective} {product type}" or "this {feature} {product}"
- NOT just brand name
- Example: "these heated knee pads" NOT "Alpha Medical"

**{BENEFIT_STATEMENT}** - Primary result achieved
- Type: Specific outcome statement
- Length: 6-10 words
- Tone: Confident, proven, measurable if possible
- Example: "Now I'm back to 5K runs. Zero pain."

**{USP_1}** - First unique selling point
- Type: Technical feature or key differentiator
- Length: 2-4 words
- Focus: What makes product different
- Example: "Self-heating technology" or "Six steel stays"

**{USP_2}** - Second unique selling point
- Type: Benefit, performance, or credibility
- Length: 3-6 words
- Focus: Complement USP #1 (different angle)
- Example: "Works even in -10°C" or "FDA registered device"

**{DISCOUNT}** - Percentage off
- Type: Number only (no % symbol needed)
- Range: 15-30 typical, 40+ clearance
- Example: 20 (for 20% off)

**{CODE}** - Discount code
- Type: Alphanumeric code
- Format: CONTEXT + NUMBER
- Length: 6-15 characters max
- Example: WARMKNEES20, OFFICE25, SENIOR20

## 🎯 BEST USED FOR

**Optimal Use Cases:**

✅ **Cold Audiences** (Prospecting new customers)
- Builds trust through peer recommendation
- Overcomes ad skepticism
- Relatable storytelling

✅ **Mobile-First Platforms**
- TikTok (native format)
- Instagram Reels (high engagement)
- Facebook Reels (growing placement)

✅ **Lower to Mid-Priced Products** ($30-$100)
- Impulse purchase range
- Low consideration time
- Quick decision-making

✅ **Relatable Pain Points**
- Common problems (pain, discomfort, frustration)
- Widely experienced (not niche)
- Emotional resonance

✅ **Building Trust & Authenticity**
- First-time brand exposure
- Skeptical audiences
- Competitive markets

**NOT Optimal For:**

❌ Complex products requiring detailed explanation
❌ High-ticket items (>$200) needing more education
❌ B2B professional services
❌ Products without visual before/after
❌ Technical/industrial products

## 🔄 ITERATION GUIDE

**If Video Underperforms (<1.5% CTR):**

1. **Change Hook** (easiest, highest impact)
   - Try different pain point angle
   - Test authority recommendation
   - Use pattern interruption ("Stop scrolling...")

2. **Change Avatar** (medium effort, high impact)
   - Test different age (±10 years)
   - Test opposite gender
   - Different setting (outdoor vs indoor)

3. **Adjust Voice** (easy, medium impact)
   - More energetic tone
   - Different gender
   - Adjust emotion

4. **Strengthen CTA** (easy, low-medium impact)
   - Add urgency ("Limited stock...")
   - Add guarantee ("Risk-free trial...")
   - Add social proof ("Join 2,000+ customers...")

5. **Increase Discount** (last resort, margin impact)
   - From 20% → 25% or 30%
   - Test bundle offers
   - Limited-time framing

**Iterate one variable at a time.** Track what changes improve CTR.

## 🎓 ADVANCED TIPS

**Psychology Hacks:**

1. **Use "I" not "You"** in script (more relatable)
   ✅ "I couldn't run..." (relatable)
   ❌ "You can't run..." (accusatory)

2. **Specific > Generic** always
   ✅ "5K runs" (specific)
   ❌ "exercise" (generic)

3. **Numbers add credibility**
   ✅ "2,000+ customers", "-10°C", "6 months"
   ❌ "Many customers", "cold weather", "a while"

4. **Show, don't just tell**
   ✅ Product visible, avatar demonstrates
   ❌ Just talking head

5. **End on action, not fade**
   ✅ "Link in bio." (direct command)
   ❌ "Check it out if you want..." (weak)

**Platform-Specific:**

**TikTok:**
- Fast hook (<2 sec ideal)
- Use trending music (check TikTok trends)
- More energetic pacing
- Native feel (less polished OK)

**Instagram Reels:**
- Hook can be slightly longer (3 sec)
- More polished aesthetic acceptable
- Captions very important (default sound off)
- Square format option (9:16 or 1:1 test)

**Facebook Reels:**
- Target older demographics works great
- Sound often on (but caption anyway)
- Longer watch time OK
- Less trend-dependent
