# ALPHA MEDICAL - VISUAL STYLE GUIDE

## Photography Style

### Overall Aesthetic
- CLEAN: Uncluttered, minimal distractions
- PROFESSIONAL: Medical-grade appearance
- TRUSTWORTHY: Inspires confidence
- PREMIUM: High-quality finish
- ACCESSIBLE: Not intimidating

### Lighting Requirements
- Type: Soft, diffused studio lighting
- Direction: Front-lit with subtle side fill
- Shadows: Minimal, soft gradients
- Highlights: Controlled, no harsh spots
- Color temperature: Neutral white (5500K)

### Background Standards
- Primary: Pure white (#ffffff)
- Alternative: Light gray gradient
- Lifestyle: Natural environments
- Avoid: Busy patterns, dark backgrounds

### Composition Rules
- Product centered in frame
- Adequate negative space (20% margins)
- 1:1 aspect ratio for e-commerce
- Multiple angles: front, 3/4, side, detail
- Show scale when relevant

### Color Accuracy
- Preserve true product colors
- No heavy color grading
- Consistent across product line
- Brand blue (#4770db) for accents only

## Product-Specific Guidelines

### Wearable Products (Braces, Supports)
- Show on model when possible
- Demonstrate adjustability
- Highlight comfort features
- Show fit and coverage area

### Devices (TENS, Massage Guns)
- Display screen/controls clearly
- Show accessories included
- Demonstrate size/portability
- Include human hand for scale

### Compression Wear
- Show compression zones
- Demonstrate stretch/flexibility
- Display on appropriate body part
- Show material texture

## Model Guidelines

### Demographics
- Seniors (65+): Active, independent, dignified
- Office Workers (30-50): Professional, productive
- Athletes (20-40): Fit, dynamic, confident

### Expressions
- Comfortable, not in pain
- Confident, empowered
- Natural, authentic
- Approachable, relatable

### Poses
- Natural, everyday activities
- Product clearly visible
- Active or recovery contexts
- Professional for office settings

### Diversity
- Include various ages
- Multiple ethnicities
- Different body types
- Gender balance

## Platform Specifications

### Shopify Product Images
- Size: 1200x1200px minimum
- Format: JPEG, WebP
- Background: White
- Multiple angles required

### Instagram Posts
- Size: 1080x1080px (square)
- Size: 1080x1350px (portrait)
- High visual impact
- Brand colors subtle

### Facebook Ads
- Size: 1200x628px (landscape)
- Size: 1080x1080px (square)
- Clear product visibility
- Text overlay space

### TikTok
- Size: 1080x1920px (vertical)
- Dynamic, energetic
- Lifestyle focused
- Authentic feel

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Resolution minimum 1200px
- [ ] Sharp focus on product
- [ ] Clean white background
- [ ] Accurate colors
- [ ] No artifacts or noise
- [ ] Proper lighting
- [ ] Brand-appropriate style
- [ ] E-commerce ready
