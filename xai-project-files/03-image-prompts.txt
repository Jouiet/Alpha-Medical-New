## Style Guidelines for All Images
- Lighting: Soft, diffused, professional studio
- Background: Clean white or neutral gradient
- Mood: Trustworthy, professional, empowering
- Quality: High resolution, sharp focus
- Composition: Product centered, balanced
- Colors: Accurate product colors, brand blue accent when appropriate
- People: Diverse, authentic, relatable expressions
- Setting: Clean, uncluttered, professional

## DO NOT Generate
- Misleading medical claims
- Before/after transformation promises
- Extreme pain or suffering imagery
- Competitor products or logos
- Unrealistic body modifications
- Medical procedures or surgery
- Prescription medication imagery
