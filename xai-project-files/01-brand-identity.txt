# ALPHA MEDICAL CARE - BRAND IDENTITY

## Company Overview
- Name: Alpha Medical Care
- Website: alphamedical.shop
- Industry: B2C Medical Equipment E-commerce
- Founded: 2025
- Location: United States

## Brand Mission
Making medical-grade recovery accessible to everyone through curated orthopedic equipment and professional-grade support devices.

## Brand Values
1. Quality First - ISO 13485 certified suppliers
2. Accessibility - Medical-grade at consumer-friendly prices
3. Trust - Transparent sourcing, verified suppliers
4. Empowerment - Education-focused, solution-oriented
5. Speed - 7-15 day guaranteed delivery

## Target Audiences
1. SENIORS (65+): Arthritis, joint pain, mobility issues
2. OFFICE WORKERS (25-55): Posture issues, back pain, desk ergonomics
3. ATHLETES (18-45): Injury prevention, recovery, performance
