## Product Photography Requirements
- White background (#ffffff)
- Studio lighting (soft, even)
- Product centered, proper composition
- Multiple angles: front, side, detail
- Show adjustable features
- Lifestyle shots: product in use
- 1:1 aspect ratio for e-commerce
- Minimum 1200x1200px resolution
