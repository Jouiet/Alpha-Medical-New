## 🚨 RISQUES & MITIGATION

### RISQUE 1: CAC trop élevé (>$50)
**Mitigation:**
- Pause ads if CAC >$60
- Optimize landing pages
- Test different ad creatives/copy
- Focus SEO/organic (free traffic)

### RISQUE 2: Conversion rate trop faible (<0.8%)
**Mitigation:**
- A/B test: Product pages, checkout
- Add: Live chat, reviews, guarantees
- Improve: Product images, descriptions
- Analyze: Heatmaps (Clarity), session recordings

### RISQUE 3: Shipping delays (>15 jours)
**Mitigation:**
- Communicate proactivement avec clients
- Set expectations: 7-15 days on product pages
- Identify: Fast shipping suppliers (3-7 days premium)
- Consider: Top 10 products → US warehouse stock

### RISQUE 4: Product quality issues
**Mitigation:**
- Order samples: Top 20 products → QC personnel
- Supplier vetting: Reviews, ratings, track record
- Generous return policy: 30-60 days
- Proactive: Replace defective products immediately
