**2. RFM Analysis (Automatic Segmentation)**

Klaviyo CDP auto-creates RFM segments:
- **Recency:** When did they last purchase?
- **Frequency:** How often do they purchase?
- **Monetary:** How much do they spend?

**Klaviyo RFM Segments (Auto-Generated):**

| Segment | Definition | Action |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| **Champions** | Frequent buyers, recent, high spend | VIP treatment, early access, exclusive offers |
| **Loyal Customers** | Regular purchases, good spend | Loyalty program perks, cross-sell |
| **Potential Loyalists** | Recent buyers, good frequency | Nurture with education, upsell |
| **At Risk** | Was frequent, now inactive | Win-back campaign, survey |
| **Hibernating** | Long time inactive, was good | Aggressive win-back, deep discounts |
| **Lost** | Long time inactive, low value | Final attempt or remove from list |

**Flow Automation (Klaviyo CDP + Shopify Flow):**

