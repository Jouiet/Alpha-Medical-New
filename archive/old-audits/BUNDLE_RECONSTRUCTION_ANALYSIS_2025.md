**2025 MARKET RESEARCH FINDINGS:**

From late 2025 healthcare e-commerce analysis:

### Key Principle #1: **SIMPLICITY WINS**
> "As a rule of thumb, the simpler you can make your deals, the better. Keep bundles simple – they can include as few as two products if it makes sense. Overwhelming customers with every product in your line can turn them off."
>
> — Shopify Product Bundling Strategy 2025

### Key Principle #2: **CONDITION-SPECIFIC BUNDLES**
> "Condition-specific equipment bundles with treatment-matched packages are emerging as a key approach. Distributors are finding success by bundling surgical, non-surgical and dental equipment under their brand to appeal to more buyers."
>
> — Medical E-commerce Trends 2025

### Key Principle #3: **CONVERSION IMPACT**
- Visual cues lift bundle conversion by **30%**
- True Classic achieved **52% conversion boost** with simplified bundles
- HiSmile: **80% of orders** are bundles (4x average cart size)

### Key Principle #4: **FOCUS > COMPREHENSIVENESS**
Market data shows:
- Simple bundles (2-4 products) outperform complex ones (5+ products)
- Customers prefer targeted solutions over "complete" systems
- Post-purchase bundling strategies work better than overwhelming initial bundles
