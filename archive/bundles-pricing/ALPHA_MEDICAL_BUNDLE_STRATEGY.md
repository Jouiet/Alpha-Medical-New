## 3. BUNDLE CREATION CRITERIA (Henderson Model Adapted)

### A. PERSONA-FIRST APPROACH ✅

- Each bundle serves a SPECIFIC patient persona
- Products selected based on patient needs
- Minimum 80% coverage of essential equipment

**Example**:
- Office Worker: Posture Corrector + Neck Support + Wrist Brace + Eye Massager
- Athlete: Knee Brace + Ankle Support + Compression Sleeve + Wrist Brace

---

### B. NO DUPLICATION RULE ✅

- Each product appears in MAX 1 bundle (exception: universal items like basic gloves)
- NO duplication within same persona
- Strategic distribution by category

**Exception**: Basic massage devices (universal pain relief - can appear in multiple bundles)

---

### C. COMPLEMENTARY LOGIC ✅

Products must serve different body parts OR different purposes:

✅ **APPROVE IF**:
- Different body parts (Knee + Back + Wrist + Neck)
- Same recovery goal (Post-Surgery: Knee Brace + Ankle Brace + Rehabilitation Gloves)
- Complete protection (Joint + Muscle + Soft Tissue)

❌ **REJECT IF**:
- 2x same body part (2 Knee Braces in same bundle)
- Incompatible uses (Athletic Knee Pad + Post-Op Rigid Brace)
- Overlapping functions (2 Posture Correctors)

---

### D. PRICE TIERS (4 Levels) ✅

Based on Alpha Medical average prices:

| Tier | Price Range | Target Persona | Products |
|------|-------------|----------------|----------|
| **TIER 1: Essential** | $200-$300 | Office Workers, Seniors | 3-4 items |
| **TIER 2: Standard** | $300-$450 | Athletes, Pain Sufferers | 4 items |
| **TIER 3: Premium** | $450-$600 | Recovery Patients, Rehab | 4-5 items |
| **TIER 4: Complete** | $600-$800 | Comprehensive Care | 5-6 items |

**Formula**: Bundle Price = (Sum of compare_at_prices) × 0.65

---

### E. 35% DISCOUNT RULE (ABSOLUTE) ✅

- **ALWAYS 35% OFF** from compare_at_price
- Applied on original retail price
- **NEVER change this rate**

**Calculation**:
```
Regular Total = Product 1 + Product 2 + Product 3 + ...
Bundle Price = Regular Total × 0.65
Savings = Regular Total × 0.35
Discount % = 35% (ALWAYS)
```
