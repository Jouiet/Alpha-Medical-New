## 📝 GROK IMAGINE BEST PRACTICES (REFERENCE)

### Techniques Utilisées dans Ces Prompts:

1. **"The same [subject]"** - Force continuité visage/personne
2. **"wearing the same [clothing]"** - Continuité vêtements
3. **"at the same [location]"** - Continuité décor
4. **"THEN [action]"** - Sépare étapes progression
5. **"showcasing [focus]"** - Guide l'attention Grok
6. **"for inspection"** - Accélère début action
7. **Avoid pronouns** - "her" → Use "left hand", "right hand"
8. **Close-up frontal shot** - Meilleur pour UGC face-to-camera
9. **Smartphone UGC style** - Look authentique (not cinematic)
10. **Realistic skin texture** - Évite aspect trop lisse/fake

### Common Mistakes to Avoid:

❌ **Vague prompts:** "office worker at desk" (generic)
✅ **Specific:** "30s, brown hair, grey sweater, white desk"

❌ **Too many actions:** "sitting, standing, holding, smiling, pointing"
✅ **2-3 max:** "holding products THEN smiling"

❌ **Pronouns:** "she holds it in her hand"
✅ **Explicit:** "holding posture corrector in left hand"

❌ **Complex backgrounds:** "modern office with plants, art, windows, shelves"
✅ **Simple:** "home office background with bookshelf"

❌ **Cinematic lighting:** "golden hour, rim light, backlight"
✅ **Natural:** "natural office lighting"
