## NEGATIVE PROMPTS (Marketing)

```
Negative: medical claims, cure promises, before/after medical comparison,
unrealistic results, misleading imagery, low quality, blurry,
text errors, watermarks, competitor logos, cluttered composition,
aggressive sales tactics visual, stock photo look, generic
```

## Platform Specifications

| Platform | Format | Size | Notes |
|----------|--------|------|-------|
| Facebook Feed | 1:1 or 1.91:1 | 1080×1080 or 1200×628 | Most versatile |
| Instagram Feed | 1:1 | 1080×1080 | Square required |
| Instagram Story | 9:16 | 1080×1920 | Vertical |
| Google Shopping | 1:1 | 1000×1000 min | No text overlay |
| Email | varies | 600×300-400 | Keep under 100KB |
