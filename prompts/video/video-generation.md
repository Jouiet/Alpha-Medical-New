## VIDEO SPECIFICATIONS

### Platform Requirements

| Platform | Format | Duration | Size |
|----------|--------|----------|------|
| TikTok | 9:16 vertical | 15-60s | 1080×1920 |
| Instagram Reels | 9:16 vertical | 15-90s | 1080×1920 |
| Instagram Feed | 1:1 or 4:5 | 3-60s | 1080×1080/1350 |
| Facebook Feed | 1:1 or 16:9 | 15-120s | 1080×1080/1920 |
| YouTube Shorts | 9:16 vertical | 15-60s | 1080×1920 |
| Website/Email | 16:9 | 10-30s | 1920×1080 |

### Quality Standards
- **Resolution:** Minimum 1080p
- **Frame Rate:** 30fps (60fps for action)
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264)
- **Audio:** Optional ambient/music (royalty-free)

---

## NEGATIVE PROMPTS (Video)

```
Negative: jerky motion, unnatural movement, distorted faces,
glitchy transitions, low frame rate, blurry footage,
unrealistic physics, uncanny valley expressions,
medical procedure footage, graphic pain imagery,
aggressive sales pitch tone, misleading before/after
```

---

## VIDEO WORKFLOW

1. **Define objective** (awareness, conversion, education)
2. **Select template** based on use case
3. **Customize prompt** with product specifics
4. **Generate with Grok Imagine or Kling**
5. **Review and iterate** if needed
6. **Add text/music** in post-production
7. **Export for platform** specifications
8. **Log results** for optimization
