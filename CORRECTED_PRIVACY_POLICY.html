7. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

This section applies to California residents only.

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have specific rights regarding their personal information:

7.1 Right to Know

You have the right to request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you in the past 12 months.

7.2 Right to Delete

You have the right to request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions under the law.

7.3 Right to Opt-Out

We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties. If our practices change, we will provide you with the right to opt-out of such sales.

7.4 Non-Discrimination

We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights. This means we will not:

  • Deny you goods or services
  • Charge you different prices or rates
  • Provide you a different level or quality of goods or services
  • Suggest that you may receive a different price or rate for goods or services

7.5 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise your CCPA rights, please contact us at:

  • Email: contact@alphamedical.shop
  • Subject line: "CCPA Rights Request"

We will verify your identity before processing your request and respond within 45 days.

7.6 Categories of Personal Information Collected

We collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Identifiers: Name, email address, postal address, phone number
  • Commercial Information: Purchase history, shopping behavior, product preferences
  • Internet Activity: Website browsing history, interaction with our website and emails
  • Geolocation Data: IP address-based location (city/state level only)

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for the following periods:

  • Account Information: Retained while your account is active, plus 3 years after account closure for legal compliance
  • Order Information: Retained for 7 years after purchase for tax, legal, and warranty purposes
  • Marketing Communications: Retained until you unsubscribe, plus 90 days for compliance verification
  • Customer Support Records: Retained for 2 years after last interaction for quality assurance
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated and anonymized after 26 months (Google Analytics 4 standard)
  • Cookie Data: Retained for 365 days maximum, as disclosed in our cookie consent banner

After the retention period expires, we securely delete or anonymize your personal information. You may request early deletion by contacting contact@alphamedical.shop.

When we transfer your data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission for EU data transfers

9.2 Third-Party Services

We use the following international service providers who may process your data:

  • Google (US): Analytics and advertising (Google Analytics 4, Google Ads)

All third-party processors are contractually required to comply with GDPR, CCPA, and applicable data protection laws.

9.3 Your Rights Regarding International Transfers

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the right to:

  • Request information about international data transfers
  • Object to transfers that do not have adequate safeguards
  • File a complaint with your local data protection authority
