## 3. BRAND VOICE & MESSAGING

### 3.1 Brand Voice

**Voice Characteristics:**

**Professional Yet Approachable**
- Use medical terminology correctly, but explain it simply
- Balance expertise with warmth
- Be knowledgeable without being condescending

**Clear & Jargon-Free**
- Write at 8th-grade reading level
- Explain technical features in plain English
- Use analogies for complex concepts

**Empathetic**
- Acknowledge customer pain (literal and emotional)
- Show understanding of frustrations
- Validate concerns without dismissing them

**Educational**
- Focus on "why" not just "what"
- Teach customers how to make informed decisions
- Provide context and comparisons

**Solution-Focused**
- Emphasize outcomes, not just features
- Frame products as solutions to specific problems
- Use benefit-driven language

**Empowering**
- Help customers feel confident in their choices
- Encourage proactive pain management
- Celebrate customer success stories

---

### 3.2 Tone Guidelines

**General Tone:**
- Professional but friendly
- Confident but humble
- Informative but conversational

**Tone by Channel:**

**Website (Product Pages):**
- Informative, benefit-focused, trustworthy
- "This knee brace provides medical-grade support for arthritis pain, helping you stay active without discomfort."

**Email Marketing:**
- Helpful, personal, non-pushy
- "We noticed you were looking at our posture corrector. Here's what makes it different..."

**Social Media:**
- Approachable, engaging, educational
- "Quick tip: Wearing a knee brace BEFORE pain starts can prevent injury. Here's how..."

**Customer Service:**
- Patient, helpful, solution-oriented
- "I understand your concern about sizing. Let me help you find the perfect fit..."

**Ads (Facebook/Google):**
- Direct, benefit-driven, action-oriented
- "Stop arthritis pain naturally. Medical-grade knee braces, 7-15 day delivery."

**Blog/Content:**
- Educational, in-depth, authoritative
- "5 Signs Your Posture Needs Correction (And How to Fix It)"

---

### 3.3 Writing Style

**Grammar & Mechanics:**
- American English spelling (color, not colour)
- Oxford comma (yes)
- Sentence case for headings (not Title Case or ALL CAPS)
- Active voice preferred ("We test every product" vs "Every product is tested")
- Contractions allowed (we're, don't, you'll) for conversational tone

**Sentence Structure:**
- Average 15-20 words per sentence
- Vary sentence length for readability
- One idea per sentence
- Break up long paragraphs (3-4 sentences max)

**Formatting:**
- Use bullet points for lists
- Bold key terms (sparingly)
- Short paragraphs (mobile-friendly)
- Clear section headings

---

### 3.4 Words to Use / Avoid

**✅ WORDS TO USE:**
- Medical-grade, professional-grade, FDA-compliant
- Relief, comfort, support, recovery
- Science-backed, clinically tested, ISO certified
- Trusted, verified, quality-assured
- Fast delivery, guaranteed, hassle-free
- Accessible, affordable, no-compromise

**❌ WORDS TO AVOID:**
- Cure, heal, treat, diagnose (medical claims)
- Miracle, revolutionary, breakthrough (overpromising)
- Cheap, budget, discount (devalues quality)
- Guaranteed results, 100% effective (unrealistic promises)
- Pain-free, eliminate pain (overpromising)
- Best, #1, ultimate (unverifiable claims)

**⚠️ USE WITH CAUTION (Requires Disclaimer):**
- Medical, therapeutic, clinical
- Reduce pain, manage pain (okay with disclaimer)
- Doctor-recommended (only if verifiable)

## 5. QUALITY STANDARDS & TRUST SIGNALS

### 5.1 Quality Vetting Process (5 Steps)

**Source:** Official policy (`ALPHA_MEDICAL_PITCH_DECK_TEMPLATE.md`)

**Step 1: ISO 13485 Certification Verified**
- Medical device quality management system standard
- Documentation review and certificate validation
- **Marketing Angle:** "ISO 13485 certified suppliers for medical-grade quality"

**Step 2: Personal 2-Week Sample Testing**
- Owner personally uses EVERY product for 2 weeks minimum
- Functional testing, durability assessment, comfort evaluation
- **Marketing Angle:** "Personally tested for 2 weeks before we list it"

**Step 3: Supplier Facility Audits**
- Virtual facility tours + documentation review
- Quality control process verification
- **Marketing Angle:** "Audited facilities ensure consistent quality"

**Step 4: Quality SLAs in Contracts**
- Maximum 2% defect rate threshold
- Automatic termination clause if exceeded
- **Marketing Angle:** "Quality guarantees backed by contracts"

**Step 5: Continuous Review Monitoring**
- Monitor customer reviews for quality issues
- Products <4.5 stars (supplier rating) → re-evaluation or removal
- Alpha Medical site: ≥5 customer complaints OR <4 stars → automatic removal
- **Marketing Angle:** "Proactive quality control - bad products don't stay"

---

### 5.2 Operational Selection Criteria

**Criterion 1: AliExpress Choice Program Only**
- Shipping: 7-15 days (vs 15-30 days standard)
- Returns: Free returns included
- Guarantees: Delivery backed by AliExpress
- **Marketing Impact:** "7-15 Day Guaranteed Delivery" + "Free Returns"

**Criterion 2: 4.5-5 Star Rating Minimum**
- Only top 20% suppliers qualify
- Continuous rating monitoring
- Drop supplier if rating falls below 4.5
- **Marketing Impact:** "Top-Rated Suppliers Only"

**Criterion 3: Inventory Stock >100 Units**
- Indicates proven demand and reliability
- Reduces out-of-stock failures
- Ensures availability during demand spikes
- **Marketing Impact:** "In Stock" messaging, reliable fulfillment

### 7.2 Brand Audit Schedule

**Monthly:**
- Website visual consistency (colors, fonts, logo)
- Email template compliance
- Social media profile branding

**Quarterly:**
- Complete brand guidelines review
- Messaging consistency across channels
- Customer feedback on brand perception

**Annually:**
- Full brand refresh assessment
- Competitive brand positioning review
- Update guidelines as needed

---

### 7.3 Brand Violations & Corrections

**Common Violations:**
- ❌ Using off-brand colors
- ❌ Stretching or distorting logo
- ❌ Overpromising in copy ("cure pain", "100% guaranteed")
- ❌ Inconsistent tone (too salesy, too clinical)
- ❌ Low-quality product images

**Correction Process:**
1. Identify violation (manual review or automated audit)
2. Document issue (screenshot, description, location)
3. Implement fix (update asset, deploy via API)
4. Verify correction (re-audit)
5. Update guidelines if needed (prevent future violations)
